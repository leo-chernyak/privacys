# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 13, 2026**

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how **ScanMy** operates, what information is (and is not) collected, how document conversion and advertising work, and how subscriptions are handled through the Apple App Store.

## Overview

**We do not require accounts, we operate no servers of our own, and we do not sell your data.**

Your documents belong to you. Everything you scan is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, view, or store your scanned documents. There are no analytics SDKs profiling you and no cross-app tracking without your explicit consent.

## Interpretation and Definitions

- **Application** — refers to ScanMy, the iOS software program provided by the Company.
- **Company** (also "we", "us", or "our") — refers to the publisher of ScanMy.
- **Device** — any device that can access the Service, such as an iPhone or iPad.
- **Service** — refers to the Application.
- **You** — the individual using the Service.

## Data Stored on Your Device

All scans, PDFs, folders, signatures, and settings are stored locally on your device. We have no access to them. If you protect the Application with a passcode, the passcode is stored on your device only.

## Face ID

If you enable app protection with Face ID, authentication is handled entirely by iOS. The Application never sees, stores, or transmits your biometric data.

## Camera and Photo Library

The camera is used only to scan documents. Photo library access is used only when you choose to import an image. Images are processed on your device and are never uploaded by us.

## Text Recognition (OCR)

Text recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Recognized text never leaves your device.

## Office Format Conversion (ConvertAPI)

When you convert a document to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or another office format, the file is transmitted securely to **ConvertAPI** (convertapi.com) for processing, and the converted result is returned to your device.

- Conversion happens **only when you explicitly use the conversion feature**.
- Files are processed transiently and deleted from ConvertAPI servers according to their retention policy.
- Please do not convert documents you are not comfortable transmitting for processing.
- ConvertAPI's privacy policy: [https://www.convertapi.com/privacy](https://www.convertapi.com/privacy)

## Notifications

Document expiry reminders are **local notifications** scheduled entirely on your device, only when you set a reminder yourself. No push-notification servers are used.

## Advertising

The free version of the Application may display advertising served by **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads):

- In the European Economic Area and the UK, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form **before any ads are shown**. You can decline personalized ads and continue using the Application with non-personalized ads.
- On iOS, we request **App Tracking Transparency (ATT)** permission before accessing the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you decline, ads remain non-personalized.
- Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses data from AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).
- The Application uses Apple's **SKAdNetwork** for privacy-preserving ad attribution.

**Purchasing the premium subscription removes all advertising.** If the current version of the Application does not display ads, no advertising data is collected and no consent prompts are shown.

## In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The Application offers auto-renewable subscriptions to unlock premium features and remove advertising. All purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit — no third-party payment or subscription SDK is used.

- **Billing and renewal:** Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- **Manage or cancel:** You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your App Store account settings. Turning off auto-renew stops future renewals; the current period remains active until it expires.
- **Free trials:** If offered, unused portions of a trial are forfeited once a subscription is purchased. Trials convert to a paid plan unless canceled at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
- **Pricing and refunds:** Prices vary by region and may change. Refunds are handled by Apple under App Store policy; we cannot issue refunds directly.
- **Purchase data:** The Application verifies your premium access using Apple's StoreKit entitlements directly on your device. We receive no personal transaction data and operate no purchase-tracking backend.
- **Legal:** Use of subscriptions is also subject to the [Apple Media Services Terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/) and the [Apple Standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).

## App Infrastructure

The Application includes Google Firebase components that may process basic device information (such as an app-instance identifier) necessary for app functionality. We do not use this data to profile you and we do not link it to your identity.

## Use of Data

To summarize:

- Your documents never leave your device, except when **you** explicitly convert a file through ConvertAPI.
- Advertising data, if ads are shown, is processed by Google under its own privacy terms and only with the consents described above.
- No data is ever sold, shared, combined, or used for behavioral profiling outside the advertising described above.

## Your Rights

Depending on your region (including GDPR in the EEA/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, and to withdraw advertising consent at any time. Advertising consent can be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Because we do not hold your data on any server, most data is deleted simply by deleting the Application.

## Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). It is rated for general audiences.
