# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 13, 2026**

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how **Tabula Rasa** operates, what information is (and is not) collected, and how subscriptions are handled through the Apple App Store.

## Overview

**We do not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information.**

The Application works entirely on your device and does not require account creation or login. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

## Interpretation and Definitions

- **Application** — refers to Tabula Rasa, the iOS and watchOS software program provided by the Company.
- **Company** (also "we", "us", or "our") — refers to the publisher of Tabula Rasa.
- **Device** — any device that can access the Service, such as an iPhone or Apple Watch.
- **Service** — refers to the Application.
- **You** — the individual using the Service.

## Data Stored on Your Device

All practice data — your custom compositions, session history, streaks, sound preferences, and settings — is stored locally on your device. We have no access to it.

## Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you enable the "Write to Apple Health" option, the Application records your completed practices as Mindful Minutes in Apple Health. This works on both iPhone and Apple Watch.

- Health data is written **only with your explicit permission**, requested through the standard iOS/watchOS permission dialog.
- The Application only **writes** mindfulness sessions; if it reads any Health data, it is used solely to display your own statistics inside the Application.
- Health data **never leaves your device or your personal iCloud Health storage**. We cannot see it, do not collect it, and never share it.
- Data written to Apple Health is managed by Apple under the [Apple Health privacy terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

## iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync (a premium feature), your custom compositions and session history are stored in **your private iCloud database (CloudKit)**, tied to your own Apple ID.

- This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple.
- We do not operate any servers and **cannot access your iCloud data** — it is visible only to you, on your devices.
- Disabling iCloud sync keeps all data local to the device.

## Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app runs independently. If both apps are installed, your custom sessions and practice history synchronize **directly between your iPhone and your Watch** using Apple's Watch Connectivity — device to device, with no external servers involved.

## Apple Music

If you choose to play your own music during a practice, the Application uses Apple's MusicKit to access your Apple Music library **on your device, with your permission**. We do not collect, store, or transmit any information about your music library or listening habits.

## Notifications

Daily practice reminders are **local notifications** scheduled entirely on your device. No push-notification servers are used.

## In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The Application offers auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase to unlock premium content. All purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit — no third-party payment or subscription SDK is used.

- **Billing and renewal:** Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- **Manage or cancel:** You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your App Store account settings. Turning off auto-renew stops future renewals; the current period remains active until it expires.
- **Free trials:** If offered, unused portions of a trial are forfeited once a subscription is purchased. Trials convert to a paid plan unless canceled at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
- **Pricing and refunds:** Prices vary by region and may change. Refunds are handled by Apple under App Store policy; we cannot issue refunds directly.
- **Purchase data:** The Application verifies your premium access using Apple's StoreKit entitlements directly on your device. We receive no personal transaction data and operate no purchase-tracking backend.
- **Legal:** Use of subscriptions is also subject to the [Apple Media Services Terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/) and the [Apple Standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).

## Use of Data

To summarize:

- We collect **nothing**. There is no backend, no analytics, no advertising identifiers.
- The only third parties involved are **Apple services** (App Store, iCloud, HealthKit, MusicKit), each operating under [Apple's own privacy terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and only with your explicit permission.
- No data is ever sold, shared, combined, or used for advertising, behavioral profiling, or cross-app tracking.

## Children's Privacy
