## Common questions

**Where are my documents stored?**
All scans are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly convert a file to an office format.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

**How do I manage or cancel my subscription?**
Open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device, or App Store → Account → Subscriptions.

**How do I search inside my scans?**
Use the search bar on the Documents screen — it matches both titles and the recognized text of every scan.

**How do smart titles work?**
New scans are named automatically from their recognized content (document type, organization, date). You can rename any document at any time — manual names are never overwritten.

**How do expiry reminders work?**
Long-press a document and choose "Set expiry reminder". ScanMy sends a local notification a week before the date you set. No servers involved.

**How do I add the Quick Scan widget?**
Long-press your Home Screen → tap "+" → search for ScanMy → add the Quick Scan widget. One tap opens the scanner.
